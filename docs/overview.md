# Project Overview

Job Discovery Browser Co-Pilot will help with safe, human-reviewed discovery of relevant jobs on company career pages.

Planned capabilities:

- Import company lists from local files.
- Track target companies and career page URLs.
- Assist with visible browser-based review of safe career pages.
- Store discovered roles in SQLite.
- Classify roles by relevance.
- Export reviewed leads for manual follow-up.

Out of scope:

- Auto-applying to jobs.
- CAPTCHA bypass.
- Proxy rotation.
- Stealth scraping.
- Automation of LinkedIn, Indeed, or other restricted job portals.
